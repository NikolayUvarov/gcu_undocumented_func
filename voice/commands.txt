# Voice commands of MIND CORE (docs/voice, issue 078): what `hear` recognizes and the shell's voice control (079) acts
# on. A line `INTENT: phrase | phrase | ...` gives the phrases of an intent; `{slot}` in a phrase stands for any word
# of that slot. A line `slot NAME: words = value, words = value, ...` lists a slot's words and what each means (the
# value defaults to the words). Phrases are spelled as the synthesizer reads them; Russian and English mix freely.
