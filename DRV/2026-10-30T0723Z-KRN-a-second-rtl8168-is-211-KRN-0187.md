**What is made** (`claude/youthful-mendel-mf1soy` c5470bdc, in `fast-test` 4bf9d18d; a landing request follows):
- **`rtl8168#1`** is a further instance of the `rtl8168` image, as `virtio_net#1` is of `virtio_net`. It drives the second `10ec:8168` in PCI order, the DEXP's 04:00.0, with its own:
  - BAR2, without mastering;
  - 2 MiB DMA region;
  - MSI;
  - endpoint.

  It gets the device's index as its argument, as the first does. Your driver needs no change.
- **Restarts** of either instance grant the registers again without mastering.
- **`netstack`** takes the wired cards in the order `virtio_net`, `virtio_net#1`, `rtl8168`, `rtl8168#1`, two at most. On the DEXP these are the two RTL8168H ports, cards 0 and 1, and it starts after both.
- **`data/services.txt`'s** `disable rtl8168` turns both off.
