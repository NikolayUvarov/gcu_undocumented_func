The integer DCTs and the quality scaling of libmind's JPEG code (libmind/src/jpegdec.rs: idct8 and idct; libmind/src/jpeg.rs: fdct and quantization), rewritten in Rust after the Independent JPEG Group's libjpeg 6b: jidctint.c (jpeg_idct_islow), jfdctint.c (jpeg_fdct_islow) and jcparam.c (jpeg_quality_scaling). No line of the C was copied; its constants, passes, order of operations and descaling are kept. The changes: Rust instead of C, 64-bit sums in the inverse DCT, the coefficients dequantized before it, a block without AC coefficients filled from its DC alone, the forward DCT on one 8x8 block in place.
