# 211-DRV-0004 — An EHCI driver, for an Intel Mac's internal keyboard and trackpad

**Type:** driver · **Owner:** `DRV` (open) · **Priority:** P2 · **Status:** open (proposed; waits for the maintainer's go-ahead) · **Blocked by:** — · **Main task:** [211](211-intel-pc-from-a-sata-ssd.md) · **Constitution:** MC-12.1

## Problem

On the MacBook Pro Retina 15" (211-PRT-0004) an external USB keyboard and mouse work through xHCI, but the internal keyboard and trackpad never appear on the xHCI controller.

`usb_host` logs `INTEL ROUTING: USB 2 1E0043 OF MASK 0`: only xHCI's USB 2 ports 1 and 2 are routed to it, and the firmware locks the rest. `devices` on the machine lists two EHCI functions, `00:1a.0` and `00:1d.0` (class `0C0320`, a 1 KiB BAR each), held by no driver, next to the xHCI function `00:14.0` held by `usb_host`. On Intel 7-series chipsets the internal devices of such Macs are expected to sit behind the EHCI controllers' rate-matching hubs; that the keyboard is there is still to be seen once a driver lists their ports. MIND Core has no EHCI driver.

## Plan

- **Kernel** (generic PCI code, `KRN`): the EHCI legacy hand-off at enumeration. That is the `USBLEGSUP` semaphore in PCI configuration space, which drivers cannot write.
- **`ehci`, a ring-3 driver:**
  - reset and run the controller;
  - the asynchronous schedule (control and bulk queue heads) and the periodic schedule (interrupt queue heads);
  - port reset;
  - the rate-matching hub (a high-speed hub) and split transactions to its full- and low-speed devices.
- **To class drivers** it serves `idl/usb.wit` as `usb_host` does. init starts a second `usb_hid` instance on it.
- **Tests:** QEMU's `usb-ehci` with a keyboard and a tablet behind a USB 2 hub, and on the MacBook Pro.

## Acceptance criteria

The MacBook Pro's internal keyboard types in the shell. The trackpad moves the pointer in `wm` as a relative mouse, without multi-touch. QEMU's EHCI suite passes.
