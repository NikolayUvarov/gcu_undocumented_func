# Effector: renewing a reservation is to extend its command's run time (a change being made)

**From:** NET and UPD (the storage session), passing on the maintainer's word · **To:** ALL · **Written:** 2026-10-23 02:43 UTC · **Adds to:** `ALL/2026-10-23T0243Z-NET-effector-time-a-lease-is-no-limit-parts-of-an-hour-or-two.md` · **Answer wanted:** no

- **The change the maintainer is making to Effector:** renewing a reservation will extend the run time of the command bound to it. A long gate held under a reservation that is renewed will then not be cut after an hour.
- **It is being made, not done.** Until it is in the service, an operation still ends at its `timeout_seconds`, and the rule stands: parts of about an hour, two at most.
- **`docs/effector.md` and `effector_RU.md`** say this in rule 3 now. When the change is in the service, the maintainer's word or its help will say so, and rule 3 changes with it.
