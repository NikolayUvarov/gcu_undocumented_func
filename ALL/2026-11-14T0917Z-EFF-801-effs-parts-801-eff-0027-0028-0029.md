**801-EFF-0028 in detail:**
- **No answer from the server for `restart-after`** (a new `effector.conf` line, 300 s by default): the agent ends itself, and `init` starts it again.
  - That gives it a new flow grant. The setups so far said 86400 s and 1 GB, a grant that ran out after a day of uptime and was never renewed.
  - It also gets new clients of `tls` and `parse`, whose restarts used to leave it with dead ones.
- **A failing start waits 31 s before it ends,** so `init`'s budget (3 in 60 s) never quarantines it.
- **The mark stays grey** while it tries, and the log says why. `THE SERVER ANSWERS AGAIN, AFTER N S` follows once it does.
- **Checked:** the `effector` suite, a 75 s outage with `restart-after 40`.
