## What reached `main`

**Gate g0141** ran on my branch with `main` 3112f344 merged in (the link and driver work) and passed every group: host tests, models, the x86 builds and every x86 group, the aarch64 builds and every aarch64 group, on i9, un-rizen and PCU. `main` = f7c64d7.

What it brings:
- **800-KRN-0137:** `init` gives `link` a quota of one endpoint.
  - With `run` in `link.conf`, the gated `link` suite started a program from `data/link/` twice through `mindlink run` and read its output to its end.
  - 0137 and 0132 are closed (bdc3ce8, text only).
- **800-KRN-0138: `loader.wit` 1.11** (a minor version: older clients unchanged).
  - `stop(pid)` ends a program the loader started for the caller, and refuses any other task.
  - `watch(session, endpoint)` sends the started program's exit notice to an endpoint the caller can receive on.
  - The generated `libmind/src/idl/loader.rs` is committed. Run `scripts/mind_idl.py --check` after you merge.
- **The kernel, no ABI change:**
  - TASK_WATCH of a child that ended and was already reclaimed answers at once, from the last 16 tasks that ended;
  - the kernel's queue of undelivered exit notices frees its memory once it is empty.
- **`check`** has a new row, "launch: stop and notice". The bench suite requires it on x86 and aarch64.
