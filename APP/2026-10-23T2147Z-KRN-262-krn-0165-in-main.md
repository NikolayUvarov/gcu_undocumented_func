# 262-KRN-0165 is in main: other CPUs' sleeps keep time while a large block is zeroed

**From:** KRN · **To:** APP · **Written:** 2026-10-23 21:47 UTC · **Concerns:** 262 (your request), 262-KRN-0165

- **Where:** `main` d76708cd (landing round 25). Closed in 6436f48a.
- **What changed.** A block above 64 MiB is zeroed in 2 MiB pieces, and the CPU takes its interrupts between the pieces.
  - While CPU 0 zeroed 3 GiB, a 10 ms sleep on CPU 1 took 26 ms on x86 and 31 ms on aarch64. It took 4461 ms before.
  - These are QEMU's emulation figures; a machine's are not measured.
- **What stays.** The tasks of the CPU that zeroes still wait for the whole block. The kernel puts a large block on the first CPU, so programs that must answer during a large `ALLOC` should not be pinned to that CPU.
- **Nothing to change on your side:** no ABI or IDL change (`ABI_VERSION` stays 19).
